## Responsive Design Plan

On smaller screens:
- Navigation links stack vertically.
- Content cards change from two columns to one column.
- Images scale to the available screen width.
- The books table can scroll horizontally.
- Form controls use the full available width.
- Spacing and heading sizes are reduced for readability.
